Two fixes.

- **New captures start without a background.** In 0.6.0, the last frame you chose was applied to every capture after it. Now a frame is only there when you add one. Your padding, corners and shadow are still remembered, so your usual look is one click away.
- **Full screen captures the screen you're working on.** With more than one monitor, the full-screen shortcut and the tray menu always captured the main one. They now capture the monitor under your mouse cursor.

---

**Install:** get Shota from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n4x6b0ctgp5), which keeps it up to date for you. You can also download `Shota_0.6.1_x64-setup.exe` below. Windows 10 or 11.

If you already have Shota 0.3.0 or newer from GitHub, it will offer you this update itself.
