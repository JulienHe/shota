Precise capture: the screen holds still while you choose, and a loupe helps you choose to the pixel.

- **The screen freezes while you select.** The moment you press the capture shortcut, Shota takes a still picture of your screen and lets you choose from that. A video, a tooltip or a hover menu can't change under your selection, and what you capture is exactly what you saw.
- **A pixel loupe beside the cursor.** It magnifies the screen under the mouse so you can place an edge to the pixel, and names the colour under the cursor (the size of your selection while you drag). **Scroll** to zoom the loupe in and out, and **right-click** to copy the colour code.
- **Faster.** The selection no longer waits for the overlay to disappear before it is captured, so the editor opens sooner.
- **Lined up to the pixel.** The frozen picture used to sit a pixel or two off the live screen, so everything seemed to shift when the overlay appeared. It now matches exactly.
