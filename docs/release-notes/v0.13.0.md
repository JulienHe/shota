Scrolling capture, text and QR reading, pins, a deeper and calmer editor, and a capture bar that works on the live screen.

**Capture**
- **Scrolling capture (beta).** Drag over a page, a chat or a document and scroll it yourself; Shota joins the pictures into one tall image, up or down, and keeps going when a video or a counter changes on the page. Auto-scroll is a button, off until you ask. Enter or Done finishes, Esc cancels.
- **The capture bar on the live screen.** A new shortcut (Alt+Print Screen) opens the bar over the live screen, so you can switch windows and scroll first. Area, Window, Fullscreen, Scrolling and Text all work from it.
- **Exact sizes.** Type a width and a height in the bar, or pick a common size (social media posts and covers, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16): a rectangle of that size follows the pointer and a click takes it.
- **Freeze the screen while selecting** (since 0.8.0) with a pixel loupe that names the colour under the cursor.
- **Copy text from an area** (Windows OCR) and read QR codes.
- **Pin a screenshot** above other windows (from the editor's ... menu).

**Editor**
- **Calmer layout.** Tools are in clusters; rectangle, ellipse and line share one button. The bottom bar keeps drag, Copy and Save; Share, Pin, export size and projects are in the ... menu.
- **Rotate and flip**, crop ratios, and your edits are remembered with the capture.
- **Arrow heads, text styles, favourite colours, frame looks and shapes**, and export at 200% to 25%.
- **Projects.** Save a capture with every shape still editable as a `.shota` file.

**Fixes**
- The overlay could flash the previous capture's picture for an instant when it opened.
- Print Screen opens on the first press, and focus returns to the app you were in.
- Saving a project with a name containing `:` or `/` no longer fails.
- Many strings that were English-only are now translated.

**Install:** get Shota from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n4x6b0ctgp5), which keeps it up to date for you. You can also download `Shota_0.13.0_x64-setup.exe` below. Windows 10 or 11.

If you already have Shota 0.3.0 or newer from GitHub, it will offer you this update itself.
