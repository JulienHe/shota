Read text off the screen, and pin screenshots where you need them.

- **A bar of capture kinds.** Press the capture shortcut and a bar at the bottom of the screen lists what you can do: **Area**, **Fullscreen**, **Window**, **Text** and **Pin**. Click one, or press its key (`F`, `Space`, `T`, `P`). Every capture starts in Area, whatever you used last.
- **Copy text or a QR code from the screen.** Choose **Text**, drag over some words, and they are on your clipboard. A QR code is read first, so a link in a QR code is copied exactly. It uses the text recognition built into Windows, so nothing is uploaded and it works offline. Windows reads the languages you have installed.
- **Pin a screenshot above everything else.** Choose **Pin**, drag over an area, and it stays on screen exactly where it was, above every other window. Drag it to move it, scroll to resize it, Ctrl+scroll to fade it, right-click to copy it, and double-click or press `Esc` to put it away. The editor has a pin button too.
- The size of your selection now shows beside the bar while you drag.

**Install:** get Shota from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n4x6b0ctgp5), which keeps it up to date for you. You can also download `Shota_0.9.0_x64-setup.exe` below. Windows 10 or 11.

If you already have Shota 0.3.0 or newer from GitHub, it will offer you this update itself.
