Frames: put a screenshot on a background, ready to post.

## Background

A new **Background** tool, at the end of the toolbar (shortcut **F**), opens a panel beside the canvas:

- **Gradients:** 30 from [WebGradients](https://webgradients.com/), one row per colour family.
- **Images:** your Windows desktop wallpaper, a blurred copy of the screenshot itself, or any picture you choose.
- **Colors:** 19 swatches, including transparent, for a PNG with just the shadow around your screenshot.
- **Padding, corners and shadow** sliders. They scale with the capture, so a frame looks the same on a small screenshot and a 4K one.

Copy and Save export the framed image at full resolution. **Reset**, at the bottom of the panel, takes the frame off.

## Remembered

- The last frame you chose carries over to every new capture, until you Reset it.
- A frame is saved with its capture, so it's still there when you reopen that capture from history.
- The workspace colour you pick by right-clicking the canvas is now kept between captures and restarts.

## Also

- Copy and Save now always export at the capture's exact size. Before, the height could come out a pixel or two short in some cases.
