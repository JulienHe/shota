Scrolling capture (beta): one picture of something longer than the screen.

- **Scroll and capture.** In the capture bar choose Scrolling (or press `S`), drag over the content, and Shota scrolls it for you and joins the pictures into one tall image that opens in the editor. It stops at the end of the page, or when you press Enter or Done. Esc cancels.
- **Or scroll yourself.** If the content does not move on its own (some apps and remote tools ignore simulated scrolling), scroll it by hand while the panel is up: Shota only looks at how the picture changes.
- **Headers and footers.** A bar stuck to the top or bottom of the page is kept once, not repeated at every seam.
- **Beta.** Pages with video, animations or content that loads as you scroll can confuse the joining; if one does, it stops with what it has rather than inventing a result. Please report the ones that do not work.

---

**Install:** get Shota from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n4x6b0ctgp5), which keeps it up to date for you. You can also download `Shota_0.11.0_x64-setup.exe` below. Windows 10 or 11.

If you already have Shota 0.3.0 or newer from GitHub, it will offer you this update itself.
