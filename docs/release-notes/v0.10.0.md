A deeper editor: turn and mirror the picture, finish it in one click, and keep the work.

- **Rotate and flip.** With the crop tool, turn the picture left or right and mirror it either way. Everything you drew moves with it. Crop also has ratio presets (1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16) as a starting point.
- **Your edits are remembered.** Crops, turns and flips are saved with the capture, so reopening it from history shows what you left. (A cropped capture used to reopen as the uncropped picture with its drawing shifted off it.)
- **Arrow heads.** Solid, outline, or an arrow at both ends.
- **Text styles.** Plain, heading, caption and outlined to start from, and a button to save your own.
- **Favourite colours.** Click the star in any colour palette to keep a colour; right-click it to remove it.
- **Looks and shapes for frames.** One click gives a whole look (clean, sunset, square post, wide, story, shadow only). A frame can also be made 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 or 9:16, with the screenshot centred in it.
- **Export size.** Copy, Save, Share and Pin can produce the picture at 200%, 75%, 50% or 25% of its size.
- **Projects.** Save a capture, with every shape still editable, as a `.shota` file, and open it later or on another PC (`...` > Save project).

**Install:** get Shota from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n4x6b0ctgp5), which keeps it up to date for you. You can also download `Shota_0.10.0_x64-setup.exe` below. Windows 10 or 11.

If you already have Shota 0.3.0 or newer from GitHub, it will offer you this update itself.
