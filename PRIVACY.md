# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 3 October 2026**

Shota is a screenshot and annotation tool that runs entirely on your computer.

## What Shota collects

Nothing.

Shota has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and
no advertising. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There
is no server to send anything to.

## What Shota stores on your computer

All of it stays on your machine, in your own user profile:

- **Screenshots you take**, together with any annotations you add, kept in a local
  history so you can reopen them later.
- **Your settings**, such as your keyboard shortcuts and the folder you last saved to.

Nothing in this list leaves your computer unless you choose to save or copy an image
somewhere yourself. You can delete any capture, or clear the whole history, from within
the app at any time.

## Network connections

The version of Shota distributed through the Microsoft Store makes **no network
requests at all**. Updates are handled by the Microsoft Store itself.

The version distributed directly from GitHub makes exactly one kind of request: it asks
GitHub whether a newer release of Shota exists, when it starts and when you choose
"Check for Updates". That request sends nothing about you or your screenshots — it
retrieves a small public file listing the latest version number. If an update is
available and you agree to install it, Shota then downloads that release from GitHub.

GitHub will see the IP address any such request comes from, as it would for any website
visit. Their handling of that is covered by the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Screen contents

Shota captures your screen when you ask it to. Those images are processed on your
computer and written to your local disk. They are never uploaded, analysed, or sent
anywhere by Shota.

When you copy a capture, it is placed on your system clipboard, which is a normal
Windows feature shared with other applications you run. When you save one, it goes
only to the folder you select.

## Children

Shota is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and the
